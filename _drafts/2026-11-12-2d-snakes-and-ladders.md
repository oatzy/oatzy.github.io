# 2D Snakes and Ladders

I was watching the first episode of season 3 of "Rings of Power", and Duran the dwarf was playing some board game that looks kind of like chess, but on a board that looks like a mountain. And also there's dice involved?

I'm sure the books go into great details about what this game is and how it's played.

But it got me thinking about a board game where you roll two dice, and one moves you horizontally, the other vertically. I was imagining a board of concentric rings like what was shown in the show, but as you move closer to the centre, you'll have less horizontal latitude.

Then I thought, well, what about just Snakes and Ladders?
