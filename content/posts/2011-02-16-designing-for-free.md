First of all, you've probably noticed the new logo. Unless you're reading this in an RSS reader. If you are, go to the blog and gaze into it's logo-y goodness.

See what I did there? I totally earned that A in GCSE Design Graphics.

I like it, at any rate.

The actual title came from when the blog was still called '*Oatzy's Blog*'. Which was a terrible title, but I couldn't for the life of me think of anything better. But I did have a subtitle idea - "*Answering the questions no-one asked*". In the end, I just thought "fuck it", and went with that.

If you were wondering about the orange, it's a kind of throwback from the website I made (no longer online) for our '[Young Enterprise](http://www.young-enterprise.org.uk/)' thing in sixth form - our *company* was called 'Purple Oranges'. But that's a whole other story.

[![](/images/designing-for-free/purple-oranges.jpg)](/images/designing-for-free/purple-oranges.jpg)

[we made beanbag chairs]

Aside from that, I've been 'hired' to make the website for Dearne Valley Swimming Club, which my mum and little sister are involved in.

[![](/images/designing-for-free/logo-redraw-edit_vectorized-darken.png)](/images/designing-for-free/logo-redraw-edit_vectorized-darken.png)

[I also re-drew and vectorised their logo at no extra cost]

Now, according to [this flowchart](http://www.shouldiworkforfree.com/), seeing as it's for my mum, I should work for free. Not that I've [ever charged](http://www.myspace.com/philosophicalsheep/blog/433294363) for building a website.

[![](/images/designing-for-free/logo.png)](/images/designing-for-free/logo.png)

[no longer his]

But I have been 'paid' this time, with a bottle of rum. And frankly I'm content with that.

And if I'm honest, I enjoy doing it anyway.

Or I did; We're past the interesting part, and it's all just adding content now. Which is dull.

[Here](http://dearnevalleyswimmingclub.info/) is the link. ~~There's only an under-construction page there at the moment~~, and I doubt you'd be interested anyway, but with each link I post to it, the page's search rank will get incrementally better - it's currently on page 3, with the old, no-longer-functioning site dominating the first page of results.
