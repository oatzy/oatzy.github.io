**Student Loans (So Far)**

This basically [graphs](http://manyeyes.alphaworks.ibm.com/manyeyes/visualizations/2-years-worth-of-student-loan-uk) what I owe already, from two years of university study

[![](/images/some-random-infographics/StudentLoanRepayment.jpg)](/images/some-random-infographics/StudentLoanRepayment.jpg)

The big dark blue are tuition fees; the light blue, maintenance loans. And the little, medium blue circles - interest!

Total? Nearly *£15,000*! Joy.

**Write Her a Song**

I got a list from some website, of songs about girls (where a first name is explicitly given). I counted occurrences of names and made this [word cloud](http://manyeyes.alphaworks.ibm.com/manyeyes/visualizations/songs-with-girls-names-in-the-titl) of popular names

[![](/images/some-random-infographics/Girl-names.jpg)](/images/some-random-infographics/Girl-names.jpg)

So Mary, Jane, Maria, Jenny and Sally are most popular names.

Obviously, this isn't comprehensive, since I only used a relatively small sample of songs. But you get the idea.

**The Big Bang**

My favourite. And this is the Doctor Who episode we're talking about ([season 5 finale](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Big_Bang_%28Doctor_Who%29)).

Basically, I plotted out the character's timelines (a la [XKCD](http://xkcd.com/657/)), using a website I can't seem to find, in an attempt to make the plot clearer. It didn't turn out exactly as I [wanted](/images/some-random-infographics/BB-draft.jpg) because the web-app had a mind of it's own. But I'm still happy with it.
