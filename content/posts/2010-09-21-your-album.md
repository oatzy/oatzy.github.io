Here's how how the game works:

1\. Go to Flickr and sort by [last seven days](http://www.flickr.com/explore/interesting/7days/). Pick the fifth image that shows up. This is your album art.

2\. Go to Wikipedia and click [random article](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special:Random). This is your band name.

3\. Go to Wikiquote and click [random page](http://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Special:Random). Use the last 3-5 words of the first quote. This is your album title.

4\. Assemble your album.
