They look something like this

[![](/images/facebook-friends/Facebook.png)](/images/facebook-friends/Facebook.png)

There's 167 of them - a little over [Dunbar's number](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dunbar_number), for what it's worth - shaded by number connections. And you've got this massive clump at the bottom and the rest more spread out. And you can split them into 3 major groups,

[![](/images/facebook-friends/Facebook-groups.png)](/images/facebook-friends/Facebook-groups.png)

with everyone else just sort of scattered. The groups are only approximates, by the way; there's overlaps, outliers, omitted nodes, etc. I didn't include the name labels for clarity.

Or you can lay it out in a more circular way,

[![](/images/facebook-friends/screenshot_005847.png)](/images/facebook-friends/screenshot_005847.png)

and again, you can see those same, vague groupings.

So that's all pretty and such.

**Want You Own Graph?**

1\) Go to Facebook, and run the app [netvizzz](http://apps.facebook.com/netvizz/)\
2\) Click "Create a gdf file from your personal network" and download\
3\) Download, install and run [Gephi](http://gephi.org/)\
4\) Import your gdf file, play with layout, colour setting, whatever.\
5\) ???\
6\) [PROFIT!](http://knowyourmeme.com/memes/profit)

Fairly straight-forward.

And as a side note, I wish I'd known about this program sooner. Then, I wouldn't have had to work out [PageRanks](https://oatzy.github.io/2010/12/07/pagerank.html) by hand.
